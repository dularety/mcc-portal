Important Instructions

  • Password Confidentiality: Confidentiality of password is solely responsibility of the candidate; take care to protect it.
  • Change Password: Candidates are advised to keep changing the Password at frequent intervals.
  • Beware of Phishing: Never share your password and do not respond to any mail which asks for your Login-ID/Password.
  • OTP Security: Do not share OTP sent to you.
  • Session Security: After finishing your work, logout and close the browser.
